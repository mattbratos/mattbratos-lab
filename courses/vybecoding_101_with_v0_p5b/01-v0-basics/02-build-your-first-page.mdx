In this chapter, you will **create your first page in v0**. You’ll write a prompt, let v0 generate a layout, and then make a few simple edits.

## Learning objectives

After this chapter, you will be able to:

- Write a clear prompt for v0
- Generate a simple landing page
- Change text and basic styles
- Regenerate or refine parts of the design

## Step 1 Open your blank project

If you still have the project from the last chapter open, use that.  
If not:

1. Go to the v0 home screen.
2. Click **New project**.
3. Choose a **blank** or simple starting point.

You should now see the editor with the **prompt panel** and **preview area**.

## Step 2 Write your first prompt

Click inside the **prompt panel** and type something like this:

```text
Create a simple landing page for "VybeCoding 101", an online mini-course that teaches beginners how to use v0. Include a big hero section, a short course description, and a call to action button.
```

Tips for prompts:

- Be **clear** about what the page is for
- Mention key **sections** you want (hero, features, call to action)
- Use normal language; no special syntax needed

## Step 3 Generate the page

1. After typing your prompt, click **Run** or **Generate**.
2. Wait a few seconds while v0 thinks.
3. Watch the **preview area** update with a new page.

You should now see a **basic landing page** for your course.

If v0 returns an error, try running again or simplifying your prompt.

## Step 4 Explore what v0 created

Spend a minute just **looking at the page**:

- What is in the **hero section**? (Usually a big title and subtitle.)
- Do you see a **button**? What does it say?
- Are there extra sections, like features or a footer?

Remember: this is just a **first draft**. You can change it.

## Step 5 Edit text directly

Try changing the text in your page:

1. Click on the **main heading** in the hero section.
2. In the right sidebar or inline editor, change the text to:
   - `VybeCoding 101 with v0`
3. Click on the **subheading** and change it to a short sentence you like, for example:  
   `Learn v0 step by step, even if you’ve never coded before.`
4. Click on the **button** and change the text to:  
   `Start learning`

Look at the preview and notice how your changes appear instantly.

## Step 6 Adjust a simple style

Let’s make one small visual change so you feel the power of the editor.

1. Click the **hero section background** or the main area behind the text.
2. In the right sidebar, look for a setting like **Background color**.
3. Change it to a soft color you like (for example, a light purple or blue).

If you cannot find the background color, try selecting a slightly different container in the layout until the right sidebar shows something like **background** or **fill**.

The goal is not perfect design, just learning where to change styles.

## Step 7 Refine with a follow-up prompt

You can ask v0 to **improve** the page with another prompt. Depending on your version of v0, this might be in the same prompt area or an inline “edit with AI” option.

Try something like:

```text
Improve the layout to look more modern and centered. Use a clean sans-serif font, more white space, and a slightly larger hero heading.
```

Then click **Run**.

Watch how the layout and spacing change. If you don’t like the result, use **Undo** or open **History** to go back.

## Step 8 Regenerate a specific section (optional)

Some versions of v0 let you regenerate just one section:

1. Select a **section** you’re not happy with (for example, the features list).
2. Look for an option like **Regenerate**, **Edit with AI**, or similar.
3. Describe what you want, for example:  
   `Create three simple bullet points that explain what this course teaches.`
4. Apply the change and review the result.

This is how you gradually shape your page without rebuilding everything.

## Simple checklist

Before moving on, make sure you have:

- [ ] Written at least **one prompt** and generated a page
- [ ] Edited **heading text**
- [ ] Changed **button text**
- [ ] Changed **one basic style** (like a background color or font size)
- [ ] Tried a **refinement prompt** or regeneration

## Common pitfalls

- **Prompt too vague**  
  If you just write “Make a nice page,” results may be random. Add details like purpose and sections.

- **Doing everything at once**  
  Change one thing at a time and see the effect.

- **Fear of regenerating**  
  Don’t be afraid to try new prompts. You can undo changes.
