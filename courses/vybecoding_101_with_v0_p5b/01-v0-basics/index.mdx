This module is all about the **basics of v0**. You will sign in, explore the workspace, build a simple page, and learn how to share it.

## What you will learn in this module

By the end of these three chapters, you will be able to:

- Sign in to v0 and navigate the main interface
- Use a simple prompt to generate a page
- Make basic edits to text, colors, and layout
- Save, rename, and share your v0 project
- Iterate on your design using better prompts

## Chapters in this module

1. **[Welcome to v0](./01-welcome-to-v0)**  
   Create your account and learn your way around the app.

2. **[Build your first page](./02-build-your-first-page)**  
   Use prompts to generate a simple landing page and tweak it.

3. **[Share and iterate](./03-share-and-iterate)**  
   Save, share, and improve your project step by step.

## Tips for this module

- Keep prompts **short and clear**
- Don’t stress about design quality at first—focus on **learning the controls**
- If you get stuck, try changing your prompt slightly and regenerate
